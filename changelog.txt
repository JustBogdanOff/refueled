+ Added the gas station
| Allows for a indirect way of fueling vehicles (vehicle needs to be selected first in the gui)
| Fluid transport compatible from the sides or bottom
+ By default, capacity is 20 buckets and transfer rate is 100mB/t
- Vehicles' gravity reduced down to intended 0.08m/s, previously was 0.2m/s
- Letting non-player entities drive no longer crashes the game
- Crashing into a mob no longer crashes the game
- Using the vehicle spawn eggs no longer crash the game
- Added a `serverconfig` subtype to the `refueled` command, only supports modifying the fuel efficiencies config though
- Vehicles now discard any non-fuel fluid left in the tank when loading into the world
+ Added `correct_yaw`, `restrict_yaw` and `roll_resist` into the serverconfig
| `correct_yaw`, when disabled suppresses head rotation updates
| `restrict_yaw`, if `correct_yaw` is enabled, restricts the passengers' head turning to 135 deg. to either side
+ `roll_resist` configures the rolling resistance for every vehicle, decreasing it results in higher turn rates
