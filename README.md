*DragN's VV: Refueled*
===
*Allowing the re-fueling of [DragN's&nbsp;Valiant&nbsp;Vehicles](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dragns-valiant-vehicles)*

---

Notable changes
---
- Smaller vehicles
- Realistic vehicle control
- Fueling and battery system
- + 2 new items to go along with it
- Collision mechanics
- Many configurable parameters
- And probably more

---

Specifics
---
**Canister**<br/>
Able to pickup, place, and hold 2 buckets of any fluid, modded or otherwise<br/>
Can also fuel (as well as siphon fuel from) the vehicles via GUI or shift-clicking with the canister in mainhand

**Battery**<br/>
Can hold up to 8.1k FE, able to be charged full by shift-using it on either type of redstone ore<br/>
Used to charge the vehicles via GUI in it's appropriate slot

**Repair**<br/>
The vehicles can be repaired only from within their GUI slot.<br/>
Taking 4 ingots of iron to repair back 9HP, or any ingot of steel to heal 6HP

**Collision** -> Blocks<br/>
Driving head onto a wall at fast speeds will simply crash your vehicle, stopping it and damaging it<br/>
Can also damage the driver in some cases

**Collision** -> Entities<br/>
However, driving through mobs hurts and knocks them back

**Controls**<br/>
Press `I` to open the vehicle's GUI which displays all necessary info about it<br/>
Holding `R` starts the car, and pressing it again stops it.<br/>
Pressing `Space` while driving aligns the vehicle with the nearest axis, allowing for straight line driving

**GUI**<br/>
Hovering over any of the units/percentages focuses on them, visually displaying it's meter in the bar on the left<br/>
Clicking either of them pins them, focusing them if none are hovered over<br/>
Also hovering over the fuel stat for a second displays a tooltip telling which type of fuel is currently in the tank

**Explosion**<br/>
When vehicles can no longer take any more damage, they explode<br/>
And if they had more than 75% fuel remaining, the explosion will also burn

**Speed**<br/>
All vehicles have differing accelerations, maximum, and even max reverse speeds, but they all benefit from modifier blocks<br/>
Blocks with the blockTag `refueled:road_blocks` are considered as such, which by default includes black and white concrete

**Pickup**<br/>
Vehicles can be picked up by shift clicking with shears (or wrench), but be weary of all the contents spilling out on the ground!
